# Trust Model

| Status | Meaning | Agent behavior |
|---|---|---|
| reviewed | Human-reviewed and reusable | May cite as local wiki evidence. |
| draft | Staged but not reviewed | May cite only as draft synthesis. |
| needs_review | Missing owner, source, freshness, or confidence | Do not promote. |
| contradictory | Conflicting evidence exists | Preserve contradiction and ask for review. |
| external_reference | External source summary | Cite original source IDs. |

No research claim is authoritative until a domain owner reviews it.
